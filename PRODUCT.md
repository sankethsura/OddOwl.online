# Product

## Register

brand

## Platform

web

## Users

People buying a distinctive object for their home in India, usually on a phone, often arriving from a shared link or a photo. They should fall for a piece before they think about buying it.

## Product Purpose

Sell the 3D-printed objects made by OddOwl's owner, who runs products, pricing and stock from the CMS.

## Positioning

Odd objects that look like one thing from across the room and another up close.

## Conversion & proof

- Primary and secondary CTA: add to bag; browse the shelf.
- The line a visitor remembers after 10 seconds: the site has a light switch.
- Belief ladder: this is unusual; it is really made and photographed; there are few of them; paying is safe.
- Proof on hand: the product photos. No testimonials or press yet, so none are shown or implied.

## Brand Personality

Nocturnal, curious, dry. A little playful, never cute. Written in the first person by the maker.

## Anti-references

Template storefronts: identical product card grids, cream backgrounds, promo pop-ups, fake urgency, invented reviews.

## Design Principles

- The object is the design. Let photos and cutouts lead, and keep the interface quiet around them.
- Show the mechanism. Day and night, on and off, far and near are things to experience, not claims to read.
- Tell the truth. Show real stock counts and never invent specs or social proof.
- Delight never blocks buying. Every flourish keeps a fast path to price and bag, and has a reduced-motion version.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Aim for WCAG 2.2 AA. Full keyboard use, including the light switch and bag. Reduced motion is respected throughout. Assumed by the build rather than confirmed by the owner.
